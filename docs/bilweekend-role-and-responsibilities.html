Role description

Purpose of the role

The Operations Manager turns enquiries into travel services that Bil Weekend can sell and deliver.

The role coordinates commercial relationships, trip planning, suppliers, travel documents and operational delivery. It also supports company administration, market representation and website upkeep.

Responsibilities

CommunicationsManage partners and stakeholders

  • Maintain working relationships with travel partners, suppliers, industry bodies, creators and the press.
  • Respond to commercial requests and follow each request through to a clear decision.
  • Prepare agreements, seasonal offers and programmes for partner distribution.

OperationsPlan and price trips

  • Turn customer and partner requests into practical itineraries.
  • Prepare quotations that apply the company's rates and commercial rules.
  • Revise itineraries and offers when requirements, availability or costs change.
  • Keep pricing and itinerary information current for the wider team.

OperationsAllocate resources and deliver tours

  • Coordinate hotels, guides, drivers, vehicles, transfers, meals and site access.
  • Delegate resources according to the itinerary, group needs and confirmed availability.
  • Confirm each supplier commitment before the trip reaches delivery.
  • Support or lead tours when the operation requires direct field responsibility.
  • Resolve operational changes and keep the affected stakeholders informed.

OperationsManage travel documents

  • Coordinate passports, visas, entry requirements and supporting documents.
  • Operate the required government portals and maintain contact with the relevant authorities.
  • Track each document request until it is complete or formally escalated.

ExhibitionsRepresent the company

  • Represent Bil Weekend at travel fairs, meetings and industry events.
  • Prepare the operational and commercial material needed for each event.
  • Record opportunities, follow up with contacts and give management clear next actions.

Office workMaintain company administration

  • Maintain company email accounts, domains, DNS records and renewals.
  • Coordinate company profiles, catalogues, print material and corporate documents.
  • Support administrative work related to registration, insurance and banking.
  • Keep published website content accurate, available and secure.
Expected standards

How the role is expected to operate

  • Ownership: carry assigned work from request to completion.
  • Accuracy: check prices, dates, names, availability and document requirements before confirmation.
  • Communication: give stakeholders timely updates and clear next actions.
  • Coordination: keep management, colleagues and suppliers aligned when plans change.
  • Commercial care: protect agreed rates, margins and payment conditions.
  • Record keeping: keep offers, confirmations, itineraries and operational notes traceable.
  • Escalation: raise risks early when they can affect cost, timing, safety or delivery.
Separate objective

Website and systems development

Website upkeep is part of the Operations Manager role.

Creating a website, a new feature, an automation or an internal platform is a separate objective.

Development work requires separate planning, delivery expectations and assessment. It does not replace the responsibilities of the Operations Manager.
