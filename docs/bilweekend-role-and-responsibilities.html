Role and Responsibilities — Operations Manager

What this post owns, who it deals with, and what falls outside it.

Prepared by M Mahdi AboAlQamachi Date prepared 9 September 2026
The role

The Operations Manager turns an enquiry into a trip that can run, and holds the commercial relationships that supply it. The post prices the trip, writes the offer, commits the hotels, guides and vehicles behind it, obtains the entry documents, and represents the company to the trade abroad.

The work is reported to management in four categories. Those four categories cover six distinct areas of ownership.

Reported as What the post owns
CommunicationsPartner and commercial relations

ExhibitionsFairs, industry bodies, creators and press

Three separate areas of ownership are reported under one word. Each is described in full below.

The post sits in Operations. It works with management, communications, customer operations and administration. A request that needs pricing, a supplier commitment or a partner decision reaches this post.

Building new software is not part of this post. Keeping the published websites correct and available is. The difference is set out in Work outside the post, below.

The role in detail

CommunicationsPartner and commercial relations

The post holds the company's inbound trade accounts and is the primary author of the offers issued to them.

  • Accounts held: Against the Compass, Macondo Expeditions, Pinto Lopes Viagens, Oriental Tours, Kanon, Nomadic Tribe, Atlaseek, Rift Valley, 365 Travel Asia, Shukran Middle East, BEFRS, Migola Travel, TravelShop Turkey, Qaflh, Viajes y Viajes, Value Group, The Gallery Travel and Orient DMC.
  • Season rate cards issued to partners for the 2026 and 2027 seasons.
  • The representative agreement that governs how a foreign operator sells Iraq through the company.
  • Guaranteed-departure programmes, published in English and in Spanish.
  • Familiarisation trips — design, hosting and follow-through, which is how a new partner account opens.

OperationsQuotation and itinerary authorship

The post prices the trip and writes the document the customer reads.

It owns the commercial model: a default markup, a bank-transfer surcharge, free-of-charge places above set party sizes, and the dinar-to-dollar conversion applied to hotel rates quoted in IQD. It also owns the day-code vocabulary the company builds routes from — ARRBG for a Baghdad arrival, NA2BG for Nasiriyah back to Baghdad, MOBKHEB for Mosul through Bakhdida to Erbil — 62 coded days that compose into a trip of any length.

Recurring work: quotations for private parties and for groups, revision after a partner's counter-proposal, and the offer document itself.

OperationsResources, suppliers and tour delivery

The post commits the company's supply before a customer pays, and maintains the rate catalogue that commitment is priced from.

  • 20 contracted hotels across ten cities, each at three star tiers, quoted in IQD or USD.
  • Three guide grades — the main English-speaking guide, the local city guide and coordinator, and a female guide for shrine access.
  • Four vehicle classes, from a standard car to a group coach.
  • 76 ticketed sites and three food tiers.

Recurring work: room blocks and B2B rate agreements with Millennium, Rotana, DDK, Ramada Plaza Mosul, Mövenpick and Baalbeck Sama; guide and driver assignment against each departure; and cross-border transfers. The post also leads tours on the ground.

OperationsTravel documents and government liaison

The post is the company's route into the Iraqi visa system, and operates the eVisa portal account directly.

It handles group passport collection, Kurdistan region visas, fast-track arrival, and the government paperwork behind a group entry. It also obtains outbound documents for staff and partners — Jordan, Egypt, the United Arab Emirates and Schengen.

ExhibitionsFairs, industry bodies, creators and press

The post represents the company outside Iraq.

  • Fairs attended or booked: FITUR Madrid, ITB Berlin, WTM London, ATM Dubai, EMITT Istanbul, Uzakrota Baku, Uzakrota Global Istanbul, the DMC dal Mondo roadshow across Italy, and FiNE Exclusive London.
  • Industry bodies: the IITC General Assembly, the SILT General Assembly, and the Relink forum.
  • Written assessment after each fair. The 2025 FITUR report covered stand placement, design execution, programming, booth circulation and staffing.
  • Creator and press relations: the Atlaseek hub, Paulina on the Road, Nora the Explorah, Kach, Dave Mani, Alex Ayling, and a Deutsche Welle enquiry on tourism in Iraq.

Office workCompany systems and administration

The post runs the company's own infrastructure.

  • Mail and accounts: provisioned the bilweekend.iq mail domain and issues Google Workspace accounts to staff.
  • Domains and DNS for bilweekend.com, rehlatalutla.com.iq and downtown-hotel.iq, including renewals.
  • Brand and print: the destination catalogue, business cards, A4 material, guide uniform artwork, and the company profile.
  • Corporate documents: company registration, travel-insurance sale authorisation, and business-account applications with banks in Jordan.
  • Website upkeep: tour and article content, spotlights, link pages, and the security and performance passes that keep the published sites correct and available.
Work outside the post

Building the systems the operation runs on

Upkeep keeps what already exists working, and belongs to the post above.

Building creates what did not exist. Everything on this page is the second kind.

Two production systems carry the operation today, and neither existed before they were built.

Odysseus — the itinerary and operations engine

202 commits: 78 features, 50 fixes, 34 records. It holds the itinerary engine end to end — a request normaliser, a route matcher, a day binder, a document generator and a Google Docs writer — together with the pricing calculator that carries the commercial model described above. It runs continuously on a phone acting as the company's always-on node.

The same engine also serves the Rehlat Al-Utla platform and its Google Sheets sourcing pipeline.
